The first Capybara desktop app (macOS): add a platform by its address, load its web in a window
of its own session, sign in through the system browser, and connect this computer as a local
device that keeps serving as a login service. The menu bar icon switches, adds and removes
platforms and pauses, resumes or disconnects this computer; the app menu and every dialog are in
Chinese or English; managed preferences can lock the platforms, turn 「连接本机」 off and control
updates, which are checked every 6 hours and offered as a restart once downloaded.
