The tray lists every device connection of this computer, one row each ("host · org · state") with
its own pause or resume, disconnect, keep-awake and 「本机」 entries, and its icon shows the worst
state (offline, then busy, paused, online). Removing a platform with one connection no longer says
"1 devices", and the widening dialog counts unreadable connections when it warns about the other
connections.
