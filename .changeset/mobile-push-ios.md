Push notifications on iOS: the APNs module (`CapybaraPush`) behind `push@1` for a build whose brand
carries APNs: the OS permission (alert, sound), the device token as lowercase hex under `push-apns`,
foreground pushes handed to the runtime (shown only when their page is not on screen) and taps
opening their profile on their path, also the tap that launched the app. `CapybaraHost` gains the
registration DELETE in the profile's page, the profile WebView's current URL and an `open` path. The
`aps-environment` entitlement comes from `Push.entitlements`, named only by a push brand's
`Brand.xcconfig`.
